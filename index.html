I wanted to tell you this

A letter for you.

I have never liked generalizations. I hate the way people put me into cages, as if I am supposed to be one particular person—whether it is about my writing, my thinking, my work, my ideas, or even my likes and dislikes.

I think people lie when they say that there is a “real version” of you somewhere inside, and that you should hold on to that version. We are social beings, and I think we naturally have different faces, different masks, different versions of ourselves. We carry them, switch between them, and sometimes even contradict ourselves. And maybe that is simply part of being human.

The idea of having one fixed identity has always felt strange to me. It almost haunts me because it doesn't feel true. We play different roles throughout our lives, and as we do, our personalities, lifestyles, choices, beliefs, and ideas keep changing. We contradict ourselves. We become hypocrites sometimes. And perhaps that too is a part of the human experience.

I behave differently with different people in my life—with my mother, my father, my sister, my friends, and with you.

Sometimes I can be fake. Sometimes I can be a little rude. And most of the time, I am probably that grumpy guy who likes to criticize everything.

But I really like the version of myself that exists when I am with you.

♡

I don't know how to categorize it, and maybe I don't even want to. I just know that this version of me feels free. At least, I don't feel like I am pretending. It is one of the versions of myself that feels the most real.

I don't like gossiping. I don't like going shopping. I don't like crowded places. But with you, I can say all of that freely. I can complain about it, frown about it, be annoyed about it, and never feel like I have to explain myself.

I really don't know how these days have passed so quickly. It feels like we have spent so little time together, and then I look at the calendar and realize that, on paper, it has been almost three years.

And three years isn't really a small amount of time, especially when you are talking about a couple.

I hope someday I can say the same thing when that number becomes 33, or 43, or even 53.

♡

Maybe that is the whole point of what I am trying to say—the phenomenon of changing, of never remaining constant.

And it applies to you too.

No, I am not talking about you becoming a completely different person. I am talking about the variables within you changing. What I felt for you a few years ago feels different from what I feel for you now. You have changed. I have changed. And so have my feelings for you.

Sometimes it is difficult to keep up with all these changes. Sometimes it is hard to understand them or cope with them. But I am trying. And I hope I keep trying for the rest of my life.

I know I am not perfect. I know my behavior isn't perfect either. But I am trying to become better.

I can't always give you the time I wish I could. Right now, there is work, there are responsibilities, and there are things in life that demand my attention. But even when I cannot give you my time, I want you to know that I am always there for you.

I will always be standing beside you, trying to make you feel better and happier.

And I know I won't be able to keep you happy all the time.

♡

There might be days when I make you cry. There might be days when I make you sad or angry. And there might even be times when I become the reason behind the emotions that make you feel worse.

But even then, I want to be there.

If I am the reason for your pain, I want to be the person who stays beside you through it. I want to be the person who helps you through it. I want to be the solution to the problems I may have caused.

Because loving you, for me, isn't about making sure you never feel anything negative. It is about being there through all of it.

Even before we were in a relationship—when we barely knew each other and had only become good friends—the first thing I would do whenever I entered a room was look for you.

And when I found you, I felt happy.

Although “happy” doesn't really describe what I felt. It was something stranger than that. Something peculiar. Something that I couldn't quite put into words.

♡

I hate crowds. I hate shopping. And yet, I still go with you.

Because you are there.

And somehow, everything with you becomes different.

Everything with you has always been the first thing I want and, somehow, the last thing I want too.

And someday, when I am no longer here, I want to believe that I will still somehow be around you.

If you see a butterfly flying around you and eventually sitting on you, maybe that's me.

If the wind starts blowing when you are feeling too hot, maybe that's me.

If a raindrop falls gently on your face, maybe that's me too.

I want to believe that even after I am gone, some little part of me will still find its way back to you—through every organic and inorganic thing around you.

Because if there is one thing I am certain of, it is this:

I love you.
And finally...
